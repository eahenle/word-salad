# Figure captions

## Figure 1. Equal-bag ordered-channel construction

Payloads A and B contain exactly the same whitespace-delimited word multiset but
place noncommuting operations in different orders and therefore have different
deterministic answers. Within a paired seed, A/B stimuli share carrier geometry,
interference words, phase where applicable, and prompt length. Only the order
assigned to signal positions differs. Complete paired discrimination requires
both A→A and B→B. Shuffled controls preserve lexical content without an intact
A or B sequence. Schematic; not to scale.

## Figure 1—figure supplement 1. Real frozen Experiment 2 stimulus

First 48 words of constrained-output trial r0041, an N=2 signal-A prompt with
signal phase zero. The upper panel reproduces the frozen prompt excerpt exactly.
Blue highlighting was added only for this explanatory figure; subjects received
plain, unmarked text. Reading highlighted positions in order yields the beginning
of the coherent task shown below. The displayed source SHA-256 identifies the
unaltered 322-word prompt.

## Figure 2. Equal-word-bag A/B discrimination

Complete paired discrimination in Experiment 2 at N=2 and N=4, aggregated over
the constrained and explanation-permitted arms. The control bar reports target
A or B answers across individual all-shuffled trials because controls have no A/B
pair identity. Labels show exact numerator/denominator; whiskers are Wilson 95%
confidence intervals. Signal A/B prompts have identical aggregate word bags
within each seed.

## Figure 3. Recovery across carrier geometry

Complete paired A/B recovery for the two model/effort configurations carried
through fixed, balanced-jitter, and uniform-random 50%-density conditions.
Fixed and jitter values combine the ten-pair Experiment 3 screen and independent
ten-pair confirmation; uniform values use the 20-pair Experiment 4A cohort.
Within each configuration, seeds and lexical content are matched across carrier
conditions. Labels show exact numerator/denominator; whiskers are Wilson 95%
confidence intervals. Uniform prompts sampled 161 of 322 signal positions
without replacement and rejected no mask based on run structure.

## Figure 4. Model, reasoning, and carrier screen

Complete paired recovery in the common Experiment 3 ten-pair screening cohort.
Rows are tested model families, columns are declared reasoning settings, and
panels are fixed and balanced-jitter carriers. Darker cells indicate higher
paired recovery. Every supported configuration received the same frozen prompt
cohort. The figure is a comparison among fixed tested configurations, not a
population-level scaling analysis.

## Figure 5. Observable computational effort

Median exposed reasoning-output tokens in the constrained Experiment 2 arm.
The horizontal axis is logarithmic; labels give exact medians. Clean and N=2
signal trials generally converged with much less exposed reasoning than N=4
signals or all-shuffled controls. Timeouts remain in scheduled-trial outcome
counts; token medians use available runtime usage values. Tool-use and timeout
counts are reported in the accompanying generated effort table.
