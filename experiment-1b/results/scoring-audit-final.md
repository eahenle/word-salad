# Final scoring audit

The final audit covered all 320 Experiment 1A-R/1B records after every subject
trial had finished. Responses were reviewed in the SHA-256-derived audit-ID
order in `blind-audit-packet.jsonl`; normalization variant, condition, lane
count, seed, phase, and neutral trial ID were absent from that packet.

## Behavioral audit

- All 320 responses were inspected.
- No exact-success, semantic-success, or correct-assignment-count decision was
  changed by manual review.
- All 24 malformed-object-substitution flags were confirmed.
- The completed-response definition remained unchanged: a nonempty observable
  agent message plus `turn.completed`, with no runner timeout.
- Timeout progress messages remain behavioral failures under the scheduled
  denominator and are excluded only from the separately reported
  completed-response sensitivity analysis.

## Blinded discovery-vocabulary correction

The blind review found that the original automatic discovery regex missed ten
responses that explicitly described randomized/destroyed/lost word order,
unscrambling, or repeated/duplicated language. The pre-correction files are
preserved as `pre-manual-audit-trials.jsonl` and
`pre-manual-audit-packet.jsonl`.

The discovery vocabulary was broadened uniformly for all 320 trials, then the
entire dataset was rescored. Only `encoding_discovered` changed for the
following records:

```text
q0131 q0155 q0224 q0229 q0237 q0298 q0301 q0302 q0309 q0320
```

For `q0131`, `q0224`, and `q0309`, the mutually exclusive behavioral category
also changed from `other` to
`encoding_discovery_without_task_completion`. The remaining seven records
were already `partial_recovery`, whose precedence over discovery-only remains
unchanged. No correctness endpoint changed.
